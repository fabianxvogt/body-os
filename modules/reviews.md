# Module: reviews (always on)

## Weekly (default Sunday evening, ~10 min)

1. `bos summary --days 7` (and the previous week for comparison). Read raw logs only to check a
   specific question.
2. Write `me/reviews/YYYY-Www.md` (≤ 40 lines):
   - **Numbers:** compact table of the week vs last week (weight mean, sleep, kcal/protein,
     substances, training done/planned, energy/mood/stress).
   - **What stood out:** 1–2 observations with evidence strength (e.g. "5 of 6 nights").
   - **Plan check:** where we are in the plan phases; due decisions.
   - **Next week's focus:** exactly one behaviour to try, plus anything due (measurements, doctor).
   - **Pantry & shopping** (pantry module): what to use up, shopping list proposal.
   - **Learning:** one knowledge link connected to this week's data.
3. Present a short chat version; commit `me/`.

## Monthly (default first Sunday)

Weekly review plus: monthly measurements, charts (via `bos dash` or generated images), progress per
goal, phase transitions (e.g. baseline → change step), re-interview prompts if goals or devices
changed, and one deeper learning topic.

## Rules

- Correlations are hypotheses; name the number of days behind them; consider confounders
  (illness, travel, weekends).
- Recommend at most one new change per week.
- Phase decisions agreed in the plan (e.g. "week 3: tobacco step 1") are presented as due, with the
  data, and decided with the user.
