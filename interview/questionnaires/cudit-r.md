# CUDIT-R (Cannabis Use Disorder Identification Test – Revised)

Adamson SJ, Kay-Lambkin FJ, Baker AL, et al. *Drug Alcohol Depend* 2010;110:137–143. Distributed freely
by the authors for clinical and research use.

Answer for the **past 6 months** (items 1–7) — scores in brackets:

1. How often do you use cannabis? never (0) · monthly or less (1) · 2–4×/month (2) · 2–3×/week (3) · 4+×/week (4)
2. How many hours were you "stoned" on a typical day when you had been using? <1 (0) · 1–2 (1) · 3–4 (2) · 5–6 (3) · 7+ (4)
3. How often did you find that you were not able to stop using once you had started? never (0) · less than monthly (1) · monthly (2) · weekly (3) · daily/almost daily (4)
4. How often did you fail to do what was normally expected of you because of using? (same scale as 3)
5. How often did you devote a great deal of your time to getting, using or recovering from cannabis? (same scale)
6. How often did you have a problem with your memory or concentration after using? (same scale)
7. How often do you use cannabis in situations that could be physically hazardous (driving, machinery, caring for children)? (same scale)
8. Have you ever thought about cutting down, or stopping, your use of cannabis? never (0) · yes, but not in the past 6 months (2) · yes, during the past 6 months (4)

**Scoring:** total 0–32. ≥ 8 = hazardous use; ≥ 12 = possible cannabis use disorder (screen, not a
diagnosis). Track the trend; discuss with the prescribing doctor where cannabis is medical.

**Agent rules:** non-judgmental; store `items`, `score`, `interpretation`; compare day 0 vs later.
