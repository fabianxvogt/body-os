# PHQ-4 (Patient Health Questionnaire-4)

Kroenke K, Spitzer RL, Williams JBW, Löwe B. *Psychosomatics* 2009;50:613–621; German population norms:
Löwe B et al. *J Affect Disord* 2010;122:86–95. PHQ instruments are free to use without permission.

**Over the last 2 weeks, how often have you been bothered by …** (0 = not at all, 1 = several days,
2 = more than half the days, 3 = nearly every day)

1. Feeling nervous, anxious or on edge
2. Not being able to stop or control worrying
3. Little interest or pleasure in doing things
4. Feeling down, depressed or hopeless

German working wording: 1 Nervosität, Ängstlichkeit oder Anspannung · 2 Nicht in der Lage sein, Sorgen zu
stoppen oder zu kontrollieren · 3 Wenig Interesse oder Freude an Tätigkeiten · 4 Niedergeschlagenheit,
Schwermut oder Hoffnungslosigkeit.

**Scoring:** total 0–12: 0–2 normal, 3–5 mild, 6–8 moderate, 9–12 severe. Anxiety subscale (1+2) ≥ 3 and
depression subscale (3+4) ≥ 3 suggest further evaluation.

**Agent rules:** store `items`, `score`, `interpretation`. Score ≥ 6 or a subscale ≥ 3 for two weeks in a
row → suggest talking to a doctor. Any thoughts of self-harm → SAFETY.md §1 immediately.
