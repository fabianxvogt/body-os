## Evidence labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `[STRONG]` | Consensus of guidelines (WHO, EFSA, DGE, ESC/ESH, AASM, ACSM …) or high-certainty meta-analyses/Cochrane reviews |
| `[MODERATE]` | Consistent systematic reviews or several good trials/cohorts, with some uncertainty or heterogeneity |
| `[WEAK]` | Small, few or mainly observational/mechanistic studies; plausible but unsettled |
| `[OPINION]` | Expert or coaching practice and common sense; reasonable defaults, not tested well |

A label rates the evidence behind a statement, not its importance. Association ≠ causation.

## Writing rules for these pages

- English, plain language, short sections, ≤ ~120 lines per page.
- Every substantive claim carries a label and, where possible, a numbered source.
- Summarise in your own words and link sources; never copy copyrighted text.
- End with **How body-os uses this** (tracked/estimated data, review question) and
  **Not medical advice — see a doctor if …** (concrete red flags).
- Neutral, non-judgmental tone on substances.
- Germany-specific facts (insurance coverage, hotlines, food law) note when they were checked
  (last full check: October 2026). Re-verify before relying on them.
